About This System

This is a lightweight attendance and team management system designed for small teams. Track daily attendance, manage work shifts, and generate comprehensive reports.

Getting Started:

  1. Add your team members in the Team section
  2. Set their shift timings and roles
  3. Mark daily attendance with work reports
  4. View daily and monthly reports for insights
